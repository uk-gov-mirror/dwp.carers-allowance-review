Request a reassessment of your Carer's Allowance overpayment

We are reassessing Carer’s Allowance overpayment decisions related to employed earnings between 10 April 2015 and 2 September 2025. This is because our guidance on averaging earnings for carers at that time was unclear.

If you were impacted, you may get a refund. Reassessing your case will not result in you owing DWP more money.

We have contacted most carers already. You can request a reassessment if you have not heard from us yet. Submitting a request does not mean that we will give you a quicker decision.

Who can ask to be reassessed

Carers who were employed at any point between 10 April 2015 and 2 September 2025 and received varying pay. For example, if they worked irregular hours or were not paid the same amount each time.

Who cannot be reassessed

Carers who were solely self-employed between 10 April 2015 and 2 September 2025 because we used different guidance to work out average earnings.

What you will need for this form

You will need your:

  • National Insurance number
  • full name and date of birth
  • current address
  • name and National Insurance number of the person you provided care for

You do not need to provide any evidence with this form.

What will happen after you submit this form

We will contact you for extra information or evidence. You may be asked to provide copies of DWP letters, your P45 or payslips from the time you got the overpayment.

Once we have the evidence, we will reassess your case. This will take some time. You do not need to contact us.

After the reassessment, we will send you a letter with our decision about your case.
